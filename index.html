Hi, I’m Jaeda! I’m a naturally nurturing, patient, and bubbly person who has always loved caring for others and helping families feel supported.

Supporting families isn’t just what I do; it’s something that comes from my heart.

It would be an honor to be part of your journey and support you as your family grows.

How I Support You

Compassionate support designed to meet your family where you are

Birth Support

Birth is such a special and life-changing experience, and you deserve to feel safe, supported, and confident as you welcome your little one into the world. My goal is to make sure you never feel alone in this journey.

As your doula, I provide continuous emotional, physical, and informational support throughout labor and birth. I’m here to offer a comforting presence, a listening ear, a helping hand, and plenty of encouragement whenever you need it. Whether this is your first baby or you’ve been through birth before, your experience matters, and I want you to feel empowered to make the choices that are right for you and your family.

Every birth is different, and I believe every family deserves compassionate, judgment-free support that honors their individual needs, preferences, and wishes.

How I support you during labor and birth

  • Emotional reassurance: Encouragement, reassurance, and a calm, comforting presence when things feel overwhelming.
  • Physical comfort: Support with breathing techniques, relaxation, position changes, movement, massage, and other comfort measures throughout labor.
  • Continuous support: A familiar, caring face by your side to help you feel supported and less alone throughout your birth experience.
  • Information and advocacy: Help understanding your options, asking questions, and communicating your preferences so you can make informed decisions with confidence.
  • Partner support: Guidance and encouragement for your partner so they can feel involved, confident, and supported, too.
  • Personalized care: Support tailored to your needs, whether you’re planning a hospital birth, a birth center experience, or a home birth.

I want you to feel comfortable being yourself with me. You can laugh, cry, ask questions, express your fears, or simply sit quietly and breathe. There is no right or wrong way to experience birth, and you never have to pretend to be okay when you need a little extra support.

My heart is in helping families feel cared for, respected, and empowered during one of the most meaningful moments of their lives. I’m here to remind you of your strength, support your choices, and help you feel as prepared and confident as possible as you meet your baby.

You don’t have to do this alone. I’m here to walk alongside you, every step of the way. ♡

Postpartum Care

Gentle guidance as you recover, bond with your baby, and adjust to life at home.

Sibling Doula

I support your older children as they welcome a new baby into the family. I help them feel included, safe, and excited during this big transition, making the arrival of your little one a joyful experience for the whole family.

Lactation Support

Breastfeeding can be beautiful, but it’s not always easy at first. Sometimes getting a good latch feels tricky or uncomfortable, and that’s completely normal. I’m here to guide you, offer hands-on tips, and support you as you and your baby find a rhythm that works. Together, we can make feeding a more comfortable and confident experience.
